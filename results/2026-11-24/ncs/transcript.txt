heading level 2, About Us
The Ministry of Labour & Employment is one of the oldest and important Ministries of the Government of India. The main responsibility of the Ministry is to protect and safeguard the interests of workers in general and those who constitute the poor, deprived and disadvantage sections of the society, in particular, with due regard to creating a healthy work environment for higher production and productivity and to develop and coordinate vocational skill training and employment services. Government’s attention is also focused on promotion of welfare and providing social security to the labour force both in organized and unorganized sectors, in tandem with the process of liberalization. These objectives are sought to be achieved through enactment and implementation of various labour laws, which regulate the terms and conditions of service and employment of workers. The State Governments are also competent to enact legislations, as labour is a subject in the concurrent list under the Constitution of India.

heading level 2, Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know about using NCS.
What is NCS?, button, expanded
region landmark, What is NCS?
The National Career Service (NCS) is a one-stop solution offering a wide range of employment and career-related services to the citizens of India, including job search, job matching, career content, counselling, and job fair information. It is an initiative of the Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India.
Who can register on the portal?, button
region landmark, Who can register on the portal?
Job Seekers, Employers, Skill Providers, Career Counsellors, Local Service Providers, Career Centers, Placement Organisations, Households, and Government Departments.
Is NCS free to use?, button
region landmark, Is NCS free to use?
Yes. NCS does not charge any fee at any stage of registration, job application, or interview processing. Jobseekers should not pay fees to employers.
Is a mobile app available?, button
region landmark, Is a mobile app available?
Yes. The NCS mobile app is available for finding and applying for jobs. It can be downloaded from the app store links on the portal home page.
How can I avoid fake NCS communication?, button
region landmark, How can I avoid fake NCS communication?
Some websites and individuals falsely claim an association with NCS or use its logo. Please verify information only on the official website,
www.ncs.gov.in, link
. You may contact NCS at support.ncs@gov.in or on the toll-free helpline 1514. Cyber fraud can be reported on 1930.
